Method

How this site handles an unpublished page

No invented content

No deck describes a Part II lecture, because no Part II lecture has published material. The topic line on the schedule is not content, and this site does not expand it into one.

No described screenshots

Several published pages are image- or screenshot-based with no recoverable text. Those pages are not described here either - only their existence is recorded where it matters.

Conflicts are shown, not resolved

Where two published pages disagree - the grading split, the leaderboard formula, the bonus cap, the A3 deliverable - both readings are printed with their source, and the digest names which page is tied to which rubric.

This deck is the site's honesty check. If a future release of the course publishes the Part II decks, this deck should be replaced by them, not amended.
