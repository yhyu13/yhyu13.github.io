NOTICE
======

This repository is a PERSONAL STUDY MIRROR of publicly published course material
for:

    CS2680 - Modern AI Systems: Agents and System Optimizations
    Harvard John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences
    Fall 2026 - Instructor: Juncheng Yang
    Official course site: https://cs2680.seas.harvard.edu/
    (also served at the vanity domain https://cs2680.com)

WHAT IS MIRRORED
----------------
`course/` contains a byte-for-byte mirror of the material the course publishes on
its own website: the lecture slide decks, the reference notes, the assignment
specifications, the final project specification, and the policy and computing-setup
pages. Each file's source URL and SHA-256 checksum is recorded in
`course/MANIFEST.json`, and the mirror was captured on 2026-10-20.

`courseware/` contains STUDY COURSEWARE that was generated from that mirror:
PowerPoint decks, HTML study pages, and Remotion video compositions. These are
derivative teaching aids produced by an independent learner. They are not the
instructor's slides and are not reviewed or endorsed by Harvard SEAS or by
Juncheng Yang.

OWNERSHIP AND LICENSING
-----------------------
All source material in `course/` is the property of Harvard SEAS and of the
course instructor. It is reproduced here for the personal study use of the
repository's owner. It is NOT licensed for redistribution, and nothing in this
repository grants a licence to re-publish it. If you want to share the material,
share the OFFICIAL URL, not this mirror.

The generated courseware in `courseware/` inherits the same restriction, because
it is derived from the source material and quotes it extensively.

No claim is made that the repository owner is enrolled in or auditing the course.
The course's own policy page states that auditing is permitted subject to school
policy and classroom space, and that no computing resources are provided to
auditors.

THIRD-PARTY COMPONENTS
----------------------
The HTML study pages use the `html-ppt` skill's asset library (MIT licensed,
Copyright (c) 2026 lewis <sudolewis@gmail.com>), vendored under
`courseware/html/assets/`. The PPTX decks use `pptxgenjs`; the Remotion project
uses `remotion`, `react` and `react-dom`. See each project's `package.json` for
its dependency list and versions.

WHAT IS NOT IN HERE
-------------------
- No exam (the course has none), no grading, no leaderboard data, no submission
  infrastructure, no course credentials.
- No assignment detail for Assignments 4 and 5, which the instructor has not
  published; the index page's published one-line summaries are reproduced.
- No published slide decks for the Part II lectures from 2026-10-19 onward; the
  course posts slides as the term runs.
- The instructor's personal research papers, which live on a separate site and
  are not course material, are deliberately excluded from the mirror.

UPSTREAM AUTHORITY
------------------
If this mirror and the official site ever disagree, the official site wins. Every
page and slide in this repository names its upstream source URL so the
authoritative version is always one click away.
